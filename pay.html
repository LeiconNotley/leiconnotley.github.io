How to pay

  1. Open your banking app.
  2. Choose Pay Anyone / Pay someone new, then PayID.
  3. Paste the PayID above and check the business name shown matches.
  4. Enter the amount and pay.

Your payment goes directly bank-to-bank via PayID/Osko. This page only shows the details — it never sees or handles your money. Please show your payment confirmation to staff.
